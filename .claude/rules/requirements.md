# Requirements

- Read the story before defining requirements. Identify missing facts, scope ambiguity, assumptions, edge cases, and invalid-input or parse-error behavior. Ask only necessary clarification questions; do not finalize requirements until the human answers when clarification is needed.
- If the story is sufficient, proceed directly to the active story's `requirements.md`. Capture functional and non-functional requirements, assumptions, constraints, error handling, acceptance criteria, and out-of-scope items.
- Make acceptance criteria specific and verifiable, including relevant generated-section behavior, read-only `check`, generated-section-only `sync`, safe updates, and deterministic Markdown output. Do not broaden the approved story.
- Present the requirements for **Approve and continue** or **Request changes** before architecture begins.
